Read the complete texts

The Vedas hold over 20,000 verses, far more than any one page can teach. Once you finish the path, these free public-domain English translations let you read every hymn:

  • Rigveda, tr. Ralph Griffith (1896)
  • Samaveda, tr. Ralph Griffith (1895)
  • White Yajurveda, tr. Griffith (1899), and Black Yajurveda, tr. A. B. Keith (1914)
  • Atharvaveda, tr. Griffith (1895)
  • The Upanishads, tr. Max Müller (1879–84)

These translations are over a century old and their English is dated. Read them alongside the explanations here.

Gods of the Vedas

The Vedic hymns address many devas, "shining ones", each tied to a power of nature or of ritual. Counts are approximate numbers of Rigveda hymns addressed to each.

The Rigveda itself says these many gods may be names of one reality: "Truth is one; the wise call it by many names" (1.164.46). Later tradition counts 33 gods: 8 Vasus, 11 Rudras, 12 Adityas, plus Indra and Prajapati (Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 3.9).
