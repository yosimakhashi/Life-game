Night gathers, and now my arc begins. It shall not end until the thirtieth night. I shall mark no deed I have not done. I shall claim no point I have not earned. I am the sword in the darkness, the alarm that wakes before dawn, the shield that guards me from my own excuses. I pledge my honour to this arc, for this night and all the nights to come.

Laws of the Realm honesty

  1. Mark only what is done. A trial half-finished is a trial not finished. Tick it when it is complete, never before.
  2. The ledger is sealed after one night. You can mark today and yesterday. Older nights are locked, so no one rewrites history.
  3. Leave proof in your raven. Write a line about what you did: the distance, the pages, the time. Your brothers read every raven.
  4. Any brother may ask for proof. A screenshot, a photo, a step count. Refusing to show it is a confession.
  5. Missing a night is no shame. Lying about it is. Zero points with honour beats 400 points stolen.
  6. The Hand of the King judges disputes. The keeper of this site may strip points that cannot be proven.
