Laws of the Realm honesty

  1. Mark only what is done. A trial half-finished is a trial not finished. Tick it when it is complete, never before.
  2. The ledger is sealed after one night. You can mark today and yesterday. Older nights are locked, so you can't rewrite history.
  3. Leave proof in your chronicle. Write a line about what you did: the pages, the squats, the time you woke. Future you will read it.
  4. No one is watching but you. That is the whole test. A tick you didn't earn only robs the person ticking it.
  5. Missing a night is no shame. Lying about it is. Zero points with honour beats 400 points stolen.
  6. A broken streak is not a broken arc. Miss a night, mark it honestly, and rise the next morning. Ninety nights is a war, not a battle.
