Rules of the 40 days

1. Show up every day — even 20 minutes counts as showing up.
2. Never miss twice in a row.
3. Done > perfect. Publish, then improve the next one.
4. Phone in another room during deep work.
5. Create before you consume. No scrolling until the work block is done.
6. Judge yourself on reps, not views. Views follow reps.
